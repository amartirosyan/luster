Lustre — webOS TV app

Unzip this archive so appinfo.json is in the folder you package
(not nested inside another lustre-webos folder).

On a PC with the webOS TV SDK / simulator (webOS 5 or newer):

  cd into the unzipped folder
  ares-package .
  ares-install --device tv com.lustre.tv_1.1.0_all.ipk
  ares-launch --device tv com.lustre.tv

If the simulator is already the default device, drop --device tv.
Arrow keys, Enter, and the color buttons match a TV remote.
Red Still, Green Slide, Yellow Portrait, Blue Prints.
5 on a keyboard selects Strip.
Up / Down changes speed, except in Prints and Strip where it zooms.
Enter or the Play key pauses. Back shows the controls, then leaves the app.
Photos opens Google Photos or pictures on this device.
The built-in reel plays without it.

Portrait is the face camera: one face zooms in and back out, two or three
faces are panned in order, four or more share one wide frame.
Photos with no face drift slowly across the middle.
